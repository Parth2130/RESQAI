About RESQAI Project

AI-Powered Emergency Management & Disaster Response System

Open Live Operations
Competition Submission

International Grand Challenge 2026 • Pune, India

Theme: AI for Climate Change
Domain: Disaster Management & Civic Resilience
Project Concept: "Transforming fragmented situational data into intelligent, coordinated, real-time rescue operations."
Geographic Focus: Pune Basin & Western Ghats Region, Maharashtra, India — an area highly vulnerable to extreme monsoon cloudbursts, dam outflow flash floods, and ghat corridor landslides.

Full-Stack System Architecture

  • Frontend: Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Clean Modular CSS3, HTML5, Lucide Icons. No heavy frameworks required.
  • Geospatial & Charts: Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap tiles and Chart.js for responsive situation reports.
  • Backend: Python 3.13+, FastAPI REST framework, Uvicorn ASGI server, Pydantic schema validation.
  • Database: SQLite with SQLAlchemy 2.0 ORM; auto-initialization and idempotent seed demonstration records.

Core Decision Engines

  • Explainable Risk Engine (0-100): Computes normalized scores weighing disaster intensity, trapped human counts, and environmental warnings, complete with uncertainty factor disclosures.
  • Intelligent Dispatch Engine: Ranks assets by disaster-type affinity (boats for floods, hydraulic tools for collapses) and great-circle Haversine proximity.
  • Hazard-Aware Routing Engine: Simulates realistic road corridor tortuosity (1.32x) and warns dispatchers when transit vectors pass near active high-severity hazard nodes.

Future Extensibility & Production Roadmap

1. IoT Catchment Telemetry Direct integration with river basin ultrasonic water depth sensors, dam discharge gates, and IMD radar feeds.
2. Multi-Lingual Citizen Voice Intake Voice intake in Marathi, Hindi, and English with automated speech-to-text and NLP extraction of trapped counts.
3. Government 112 / NDRF CAD Gateway Secure authenticated integration into official state disaster response computer-aided dispatch (CAD) infrastructure.

Important Real-World Operational Limitations & Ethics

RESQAI is an academic competition demonstration prototype developed for the International Grand Challenge 2026. The initial release is a rule-based decision support tool and is not a clinically or operationally validated national disaster response engine.

  • Does not claim live connection to official national disaster databases without authorized agency credentials.
  • Transit route recommendations are calculated via waypoint interpolation with simulated road network tortuosity and do not replace driver reconnaissance of live flash floods or road blockages.
  • AI asset suggestions require mandatory human officer review and explicit confirmation before dispatching field crews.
  • No actual SMS or emergency calls are dispatched from this local demo environment.
