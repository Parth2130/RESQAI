Integrated Capabilities

Everything Emergency Responders Need

Transforming fragmented situational data into intelligent, coordinated, real-time rescue operations.

Intelligent Risk Assessment

Transparent, explainable risk scoring (0-100) weighing hazard category, reported severity, people needing extraction, and weather triggers.

Interactive Disaster Map

Real-time geospatial visualization via Leaflet.js with risk-coded hazard pins, shelter markers, active staging units, and filter layers.

Rescue Resource Coordination

Comprehensive asset directory tracking ambulances, inflatable rescue boats, NDRF search units, and fire crews with status controls.

Hazard-Aware Routing

Calculates realistic road corridors and flags active high-severity hazard nodes intersecting the dispatch path to safeguard rescue crews.

Shelter & Vulnerability Tracking

Live capacity monitoring and occupancy warnings with aggregated counts for elderly citizens, children, and people with mobility needs.

Analytics & Situation Reports

Data-driven situation reports via Chart.js detailing incident types, severity spreads, resource saturation, and CSV export for officers.

Standard Operating Procedure

How RESQAI Coordinates Incident Lifecycles

1

Collect Data

Public or responder submits verified location, hazard type, and trapped headcounts.

2

Assess Risk

Algorithm weighs severity, vulnerability, and weather triggers to assign 0-100 score.

3

Rank Assets

Engine computes capability affinity & haversine distance to suggest optimal units.

4

Authorize Dispatch

Human commander reviews corridor hazards and authorizes team deployment.

5

Track & Resolve

Live status transitions from En Route to Resolved; units safely released to pool.

Operational Expected Impact

  • Reduces triage latency by prioritizing critical extraction emergencies over routine inquiries.
  • Prevents resource misallocation by matching specific rescue capabilities (boats for floods, hydraulic cutters for collapsed structures).
  • Protects frontline teams by warning dispatchers when proposed routes cross active hazard zones.

Competition Submission Context

Developed for the International Grand Challenge 2026 in Pune, India under the theme AI for Climate Change (Disaster Management Domain).

Focuses on climate-induced extreme meteorological events (flash floods, ghat landslides, structural inundations) prevalent across Western Maharashtra and developing river basins.
